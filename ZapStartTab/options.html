How to Use

A
Quick Open

Press any key to instantly open its primary link.

A
Hold & Select

Hold a key to show the link panel, then use your mouse to pick a secondary link before releasing.

Shift
Shift + Key

Hold Shift to reveal the keyboard overlay, then press a key. Release Shift to open the selected link.

Right-click
Edit Shortcuts

Shift + right-click any key to edit its primary and secondary links.

Esc
Cancel

Press Escape to close the link panel or the edit modal without navigating.
